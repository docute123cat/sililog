I'm an engineer changing careers into **embedded systems**.

Right now I'm working through bare-metal firmware on STM32 — writing drivers
without HAL, one peripheral at a time. After that: Linux/BSP (kernel drivers,
Device Tree, Yocto), which is where I want to specialize.

This blog is my **build log**. Each post follows the same shape: what I was
trying to do, what broke, how I debugged it, what the actual bug was, and what
I learned. I write them mostly so I stop making the same mistake twice — but
if they save you an evening of debugging, even better.

Everything here is written while holding a full-time job, on nights and
weekends. Progress is slow and that's fine.

- **GitHub:** [docute123cat](https://github.com/docute123cat)
- **Currently learning:** STM32 bare-metal, FreeRTOS
- **Next up:** Embedded Linux, kernel drivers, Yocto
